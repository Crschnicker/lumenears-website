What it is

Ears that come alive

LumenEars is a wearable headband with two round, full-color displays where classic ears would be. Instead of a static shape, each ear is a little window: characters walk, blink, and play across them in real time.

The ears react to you. Tilt your head, shake it, or turn around and the scene answers. Point your phone browser at the headband and you have a controller — no app store, nothing to install — for switching worlds and starting games with friends wearing theirs.

  • Two round, high-resolution screens with a real-time animation engine
  • 8 animated backgrounds and characters included at launch
  • Motion-reactive: tilt, shake and head tracking
  • Rechargeable LiPo battery over USB-C
  • New worlds and features arrive over WiFi after you get it

How it works

Simple to wear. Deep to play.

Everything runs on the headband. Your phone is just the remote.

Wireless controller

Open your phone browser and you are connected. Nothing to install, no account, no app store — switch worlds, start games, change settings.

Motion-reactive

Built-in motion sensing picks up tilt, shake and head tracking, so the characters answer the way you move instead of looping the same clip.

Rechargeable

A protected LiPo cell charges over standard USB-C. No proprietary dock, no batteries to hunt down mid-day.

Risks, honesty, and the fine print

A LumenEars headband with two round, full-color screens, the full launch cast of 8 animated backgrounds and characters, the phone-browser controller, and every stretch goal unlocked during the campaign. Hardware tiers start at $189.

We've carried LumenEars from a bare circuit board to working, motion-reactive hardware — which means we've already met most of the hard problems. Here's an honest look at what's left and how we're handling it.

Certification. Wireless wearables need FCC/CE testing, which takes time and can require small board revisions. We've budgeted for a full test cycle plus one revision in both the schedule and the funding goal.

Tooling and fit. Turning our prototype housing into finalized production-ready designs is hard work. Part of this raise pays for proper tooling and a comfort/fit pass before mass production.

Battery safety. We use a protected LiPo cell and standard USB-C charging, and battery packs will be certified to the appropriate transport and safety standards before they ship.

Software polish. The engine works; the last mile is stability, tuning motion feel, and working on the party games side of the project. Firmware updates arrive over WiFi, so improvements reach your ears after delivery, too.

Fulfillment. We're planning realistic shipping windows based on the expected timeline to completion.

We used AI-assisted tools during marketing and development around the concept to give users a tangible product visual. Visuals generated — including product renders and character art — were created using AI tools, then selected and refined by our team. Actual animations are created manually, to take the concept and implement it in our current prototypes for testing.

The goal is to help people see the goal of our product, not to generate unrealistic expectations. We already have a physical prototype of the product with animations and games, and want to turn it into something everyone can love and enjoy.
