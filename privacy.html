Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 August 2026

The short version

This site has no forms, no sign-up, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. We do not ask you for anything and we do not set any cookies. Two things still reach third parties when you visit: the web font files, and a video embed if you press play. Both are explained below.

1. What we do not collect

We want to be precise about this, because most privacy policies are vague where it matters. On this Site there is:

  • no contact form, newsletter form or email capture of any kind;
  • no account, login or profile;
  • no analytics, no tracking pixel, no advertising network, no session recording;
  • no cookie set by us, and no use of local storage or session storage;
  • no payment processing — we never see a card number, because pledging happens on Kickstarter.

There is therefore no marketing database behind this Site, and nothing for us to sell or share. We do not sell or share personal information, and we never have.

2. What happens automatically when you visit

Server logs

Like any website, this Site is served by a hosting provider that may keep standard access logs, typically the requesting IP address, the time of the request, the page or file requested, the referring page and the browser's user-agent string. These logs exist to serve the pages, to keep the service secure and to diagnose faults. Our hosting provider is [HOSTING PROVIDER] and its retention period is [RETENTION PERIOD].

Web fonts

The Site loads its typeface from Google Fonts. This means your browser makes a request to Google's servers (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) when the page loads, and Google receives your IP address and user-agent as part of that request. We do not control what Google does with it; Google's privacy policy applies. No cookie is set by the font request itself.

If you would rather this did not happen at all, the font can be self-hosted, which removes the third-party request entirely.

The hero video

The looping video on the home page is served from this Site's own server. Playing it sends nothing to any third party.

3. The campaign video, if you press play

Where a campaign video is embedded from YouTube or Vimeo, it is click-to-load: the page shows a still image, and nothing is requested from YouTube or Vimeo until you press play. That means no third-party script and no third-party cookie reaches you unless you choose to watch.

If you do press play, the player loads from youtube-nocookie.com or player.vimeo.com and that provider then receives your IP address and may set cookies or similar identifiers under its own privacy policy. Not pressing play avoids this completely.

4. If you email us

If you write to eesha@rclick.com we receive your email address, your name if you give it and whatever you put in the message. We use it only to answer you, we keep it no longer than we need it for that purpose, and we do not add you to any mailing list.

5. When you leave for Kickstarter or social media

The buttons on this Site take you to Kickstarter, and the footer links to Instagram and TikTok. Those are separate services with their own privacy policies and their own tracking. Anything you do there — including backing the project, which involves giving Kickstarter your name, address and payment details — is between you and them. We only receive what Kickstarter chooses to share with a project creator, which for reward fulfilment normally includes the backer's name, email address and shipping address.

6. Children

LumenEars is a product that appeals to children, but this Site is aimed at the adults who back campaigns, and it is not directed at children under 13. Because the Site collects nothing at all, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, write to us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict its processing, to receive a copy of it, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. This includes rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended (CCPA/CPRA) and, for visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the GDPR.

In practice, the only personal information we are likely to hold is an email exchange you started. To exercise any of these rights, write to eesha@rclick.com and we will respond within the period the applicable law allows.

Where the GDPR applies, our lawful basis for keeping server logs and for replying to your email is our legitimate interest in operating a secure website and in answering the people who contact us.

8. Security and international transfers

The Site is served over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but since the Site holds no accounts and no personal data, there is very little to expose. Our hosting provider and the font and video services named above may process requests on servers in the United States and elsewhere, so visiting the Site may involve an international transfer of the technical data described in section 2.

9. Changes to this policy

If the Site changes — for example if a signup form or analytics is ever added — this policy will be updated before that change goes live, and the date at the top of this page will tell you when it last changed.

10. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints about privacy go to eesha@rclick.com.
